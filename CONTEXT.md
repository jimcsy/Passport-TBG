# BEAT learning milestones

The portal organizes training competencies into development paths and preserves
trainer verification as trainees' plans evolve.

## Language

**Milestone**:
A competency that a trainee must demonstrate and a trainer verifies.
_Avoid_: Course, automatic completion

**Pillar**:
One of the five core development-path categories used to organize milestones.

**Verification criteria**:
Observable evidence a trainer uses to decide whether a trainee has demonstrated
the milestone's competency.

**Strobi proposal**:
An unsaved suggestion for milestone wording and a recommended pillar, reviewed
and editable by the trainer before creating the milestone.
_Avoid_: Published milestone, saved milestone, working draft

**Working draft**:
The editable version of an existing training plan before its publication.
_Avoid_: Strobi proposal

**Strobi tip**:
Learning guidance for a trainee's selected milestone, explaining the skill,
suggesting practice, illustrating an example or helping prepare for trainer review.
_Avoid_: Strobi proposal, verification, earned stamp
